Abacus Proposal Management

An internal business application used by authorised Abacus personnel to manage customer records, prepare and track commercial proposals, create agreements, coordinate onboarding, and archive business documents.

Open staff application Privacy policy Terms of use

What the application does

Customer workspace

Maintains business customer profiles and related contact and tax-registration details for proposal and agreement workflows.

Proposal management

Prepares, issues, revises and tracks commercial proposals, including subscription volumes, pricing, scope and status history.

Agreements and onboarding

Creates agreements from accepted proposals and records onboarding information and related documents.

Document archive

Generates PDF records and, when an administrator connects Google Drive, archives and retrieves the specific files used by the application.

Google Drive connection

An authorised administrator may connect the company Google account so the application can create and manage its proposal, agreement and onboarding archive files. The application requests Google's drive.file permission, which is limited to the specific Drive files used with this application. It does not request general access to every file in the account.

Details about the information involved and how the Drive connection is handled are in the Privacy Policy.

Who can use it

The application is intended for approved Abacus staff. It is not a public customer portal. Staff access is controlled through individual usernames, passwords and assigned roles or business-unit permissions.

Customers and other business contacts should contact their Abacus representative rather than attempting to create an application account.
