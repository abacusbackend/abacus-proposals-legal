Privacy Policy

Abacus Proposal Management · Effective date: 21 November 2026 · Last updated: 21 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Abacus Accounting LLC (“Abacus”, “we”, “us” or “our”) handles information through the Abacus Proposal Management application (“Application”) and this public information website.

The Application is an internal, access-controlled business tool for authorised Abacus personnel. It supports customer records, commercial proposals, agreements, onboarding workflows and document archiving. It is not intended to be a public customer account portal.

Privacy contact: abacus.backend@gmail.com

1. Who is responsible for the information?

Abacus Accounting LLC operates the Application and determines how it is used for its internal proposal and agreement workflows. For information about a customer or an individual contact entered by an Abacus team member, Abacus will handle that information in accordance with the relevant customer relationship and applicable law. Where another Abacus business unit or a customer determines the purpose of a particular processing activity, the parties’ applicable agreement may further describe their respective responsibilities.

2. Information we handle

The types of information depend on how the Application is used. They may include:

  • Staff account information: username, staff name, account status, role and assigned business-unit or company access. Staff authenticate using a username and password. Authentication is provided through Supabase Auth; the Application does not display staff passwords to other staff.
  • Customer business and contact information: customer legal name and trade name, tax registration number (TRN), business address, business email and telephone number, and contact-person details.
  • Commercial and service records: proposal and agreement numbers, document volumes, subscription prices, onboarding fees, additional services, overage terms, service scope, proposal and agreement status, revision reasons and related correspondence or notes entered into the Application.
  • Onboarding and document information: onboarding records, uploaded or generated PDFs and other business documents, document names, file types, file sizes, Drive file identifiers and upload or archive timestamps.
  • Activity and audit information: staff identifiers, event types, status changes, revision and workflow history, pricing-change records and timestamps. These records support accountability and business recordkeeping.
  • Technical and session information: authentication/session tokens held in the browser’s session storage while a staff session is active, and technical request, error and service logs generated by the Application and its hosting providers. Infrastructure providers may also process IP address, browser/device and connection metadata when their services are used.

Please do not enter sensitive personal information into the Application unless it is genuinely required for the relevant business workflow and you are authorised to provide it.

3. Why we use the information

We use information only as reasonably needed to:

  • authenticate staff and apply role- and business-unit-based access;
  • create and maintain business customer profiles;
  • prepare, issue, revise and track proposals and agreements;
  • manage onboarding activities and supporting records;
  • generate, archive, preview and retrieve business documents;
  • maintain workflow histories, auditability, accounting and commercial records;
  • secure, troubleshoot, maintain and improve the Application; and
  • respond to support, privacy and legal requests.

4. Google user data and Google Drive

An administrator may connect one company Google account to enable the Application’s document archive. The Application requests the Google Drive drive.file OAuth scope. Google describes this scope as access to the specific Drive files that a user uses with the application. The Application does not request broad access to the account’s entire Google Drive.

When the administrator authorises the connection, the Application uses Google OAuth credentials to create and manage the archive folder and to create, upload, read or retrieve the specific proposal, agreement and onboarding files handled by the Application. The connected account’s authorisation is used for these document-management functions, not for advertising.

The Application stores the Drive refresh token in encrypted form in its Supabase database. The token is encrypted by the Application using AES-GCM before it is saved. The Google OAuth client credentials and token-encryption key are held as server-side Cloudflare Worker secrets. Short-lived access tokens are obtained when the Application needs to communicate with Google Drive.

Google Drive data is used only to provide the Application’s document-archive features. We do not sell Google user data, use it for advertising, or use it to train general-purpose artificial-intelligence or machine-learning models. We do not transfer it to third parties except as necessary to provide the Application’s service, comply with law, protect the service, or as otherwise permitted by the Google API Services User Data Policy and applicable law.

You can review or revoke the connected account’s Google permissions through your Google Account third-party access settings. Revoking access may stop the Application’s Drive archive functions until an administrator reconnects the account.

5. Where information is stored and service providers

The Application uses service providers to operate its features:

  • Cloudflare: hosts the Application as a Worker and provides browser-based document/PDF processing, runtime and service logs.
  • Supabase: provides the database and staff authentication services. The current primary database project is configured in the ap-south-1 (Mumbai, India) region.
  • Google: provides the authorised Google Drive storage and APIs for the business document archive.
  • GitHub Pages: hosts this public information website. GitHub may process visitor IP addresses and related request information for the security and operation of its Pages service.

These providers may process information in locations outside the United Arab Emirates, subject to their service infrastructure and applicable terms. The database region does not mean that every provider or every processing operation is limited to that location. We do not control the providers’ independent security and privacy practices; please review their policies:

  • Cloudflare Privacy Policy
  • Supabase Privacy Policy
  • Google Privacy Policy
  • GitHub Privacy Statement

6. When information may be shared

We do not sell personal information. We share or make information available only where reasonably necessary for the purposes in this Policy, including with the infrastructure providers listed above, authorised Abacus personnel who need access for their role, professional advisers where appropriate, or competent authorities where disclosure is required by law or necessary to protect legal rights and the security of the Application.

Access to business records within the Application is controlled through staff authentication, role permissions and assigned business-unit access. Do not share your staff credentials with another person.

7. Retention

We retain information for as long as it is reasonably needed to operate the relevant customer, proposal, agreement, onboarding and archive workflows, maintain business and audit history, resolve disputes, meet applicable legal or accounting obligations, and protect the Application. Some historical proposal, agreement and activity records may be retained even when a record is marked as archived or deleted in the user interface. The Application does not publish a single fixed retention period for every record type; retention depends on the type of record, the business purpose and applicable requirements.

When information is no longer required, we will take appropriate steps to delete it or otherwise securely dispose of it, subject to legal, contractual and legitimate recordkeeping requirements and the capabilities of the relevant service providers.

8. Security

We use controls appropriate to an internal business application, including authenticated staff accounts, role and business-unit access checks, server-side handling of privileged database credentials, encrypted storage of the Google Drive refresh token, and infrastructure security and logging features provided by our service providers. No website, network or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Staff must protect their passwords and promptly report suspected unauthorised access.
